In this guide, we cover how to integrate the Atomy Air Purifier (model `AAP-KR19W`) into Home Assistant for fast, local control without cloud latency.

---

## 🛠️ Key Details

- **Device**: Atomy Air Purifier (`AAP-KR19W`)
- **Protocol**: Tuya Wi-Fi / Local Tuya Integration
- **Dust Sensor**: Laser PM2.5 Dust Sensor (`PMS9003M` / `ZH03B`)

### DP Mapping Table
- Power State: `1` (1 = OFF, 2 = ON)
- Fan Speed: `12`
- PM2.5 Reading: `13`
- Air Quality Level: `26`
